GIFmaker Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

GIFmaker is designed as a local-first GIF creation app. The app does not require an account and does not collect personal data.

Photos

GIFmaker asks permission to select photos and to save exported GIFs. Selected photos are processed on your device to create the GIF file.

Data Collection

GIFmaker does not collect, sell, or share personal data. GIF files are created locally and are only shared when you choose to use the system share sheet or save them to Photos.

Tracking

GIFmaker does not track you across apps or websites.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact: support@example.com
